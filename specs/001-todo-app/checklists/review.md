## Requirement quality

- [x] **CHK001 — Observable and technology-neutral requirements.** Product requirements describe user-visible behavior rather than prescribing implementation. **Evidence:** `spec.md` § Requirements, FR-001–FR-016. Platform and stack are assigned to the plan. Authentication UX and invitation mechanics remain explicitly unspecified in `spec.md` § Clarifications and § Scope Exclusions.
- [x] **CHK002 — Specific, testable requirements and validation.** Requirements identify actors, actions, values, permissions, and observable outcomes. **Evidence:** FR-001–FR-016; FR-010–FR-011 specify nonblank title, exactly one responsible member, and required High/Medium/Low priority; User Stories 1–3 acceptance scenarios state outcomes.
- [x] **CHK003 — Lifecycle and persistence consistency.** Persistence of tasks not removed is distinguished from removal and unresolved retention mechanics. **Evidence:** `spec.md` FR-007, FR-009, § Edge Cases, and SC-004; approved `plan.md` v3 § Summary and § Proposed Architecture and Decisions item 4; approved `data-model.md` v3 § `tasks`. They preserve the same boundary without selecting physical deletion, soft deletion, retention duration, or recovery.
- [x] **CHK004 — Coverage of core family-list behavior and access boundaries.** Creation, shared visibility, assignment, priority, completion, membership, and roles are covered. **Evidence:** `spec.md` FR-001–FR-016; User Stories 1–3 and acceptance scenarios; SC-006–SC-008; approved plan v3 § Summary and § Proposed Architecture and Decisions items 3, 6, and 7; approved data-model v3 § Invariants and access rules.
- [x] **CHK005 — User scenarios and acceptance outcomes.** Prioritized stories state user value, independent test intent, and Given/When/Then outcomes. **Evidence:** `spec.md` § User Scenarios & Testing, User Stories 1–3 and acceptance scenarios cover task creation/viewing, assignment/priority, completion, default Done visibility, and permissions.
- [x] **CHK006 — Success criteria are verifiable and tied to product outcomes.** Outcomes include measurable usability thresholds and discrete persistence, permission, membership, and default-view checks. **Evidence:** `spec.md` § Success Criteria, SC-001–SC-008. Plan v3 § Testing and quickstart v3 § Verification checklist map proposed checks to behavior and explicitly do not claim tests were run.
- [x] **CHK007 — Scope, assumptions, and decisions are explicit.** Confirmed behavior, assumptions, owner-delegated proposals, and exclusions are distinguished; unresolved mechanics are not presented as settled. **Evidence:** `spec.md` § Clarifications, § Assumptions, and § Scope Exclusions. Submitted answers establish one shared list; all members can view it; admins create/edit/remove tasks and add/invite members; all members can mark tasks Done; an external owner/support process assigns the initial admin; and Done tasks appear by default. The answer “you decide” for task names/colors is represented as a clearly identified owner-delegated presentation proposal in spec § Clarifications and FR-014, not as an unanswered decision. Plan v3 and data-model v3 keep authentication UX, invitation acceptance, provisioning details, and removal retention open. Workspace Questions retain `email-signin-flow`, `member-invitation-flow`, and `removed-task-retention` for the implementation stage.
- [x] **CHK008 — Constitution alignment.** The feature supports family usefulness, simplicity, priority focus, inclusive use, and verifiable outcomes without assuming a family structure. **Evidence:** approved constitution v1 Principles I–V; `spec.md` User Stories 1–3, SC-001–SC-008, and § Assumptions; approved plan v3 § Constitution Check describes proposed verification and identifies implementation verification as pending.
